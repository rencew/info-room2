What You Can Do

💬 Student & Teacher Forum

Share ideas, code, feedback, and questions. Posts are moderated for quality.

📝 Comments & Media

Comment on posts and attach images, videos, or files to your messages.

🏆 Earn Points

Participate in the forum and earn points that can be used in the cosmetics shop.

🎨 Customize

After logging in, customize your character with different cosmetics and themes.

How It Works

1️⃣ Sign Up

Create a Student or Teacher account and upload verification (COE or Faculty ID).

2️⃣ Get Approved

An administrator will review your registration before you can access the forum.

3️⃣ Join & Share

Once approved, log in, post (teachers auto-approved), comment, and customize your character.
